What we heard

Before proposing anything, here's what we took away. If any of this is off, it changes the design.

Reps need a queue, not a list

The goal is for every rep to wake up to the next best thing to do, across calls, texts, emails, and mail. Today they choose their own path through lists, and land at about 80 activities a day against a goal of 200.

You've built real assets

The ClickHouse bring-your-own-schema pipeline and your brand-specific models are working. Anything new should build on them, not replace them.

You sell outcomes now

Acquisition, Win-back, Retain, and Grow. Reporting has to answer "are we doing a good job?" by product, not by counting records touched.

No more light switches

Two all-at-once migrations failed. The next one runs in parallel and grows one workflow and one group of reps at a time, proving scale at 50, 100, and 200.

A rep's day, before and after

The productivity gap is easiest to see from a single rep's chair.

Today

  1. Opens HubSpot and picks an ABM list to work
  2. Decides which accounts to call first
  3. Switches to Aircall to dial, then back to log the outcome
  4. Can't text at scale without a compliance layer
  5. Call data reaches the database only partly, through APIs and scrapers

About 80 activities a daySignals from your models never reach the rep's next move

Proposed

  1. Opens a work queue already ranked by your signals
  2. Each item shows the channel and the reason it's next
  3. Calls, texts, or emails from the same screen
  4. Outcome and transcript land on the record automatically
  5. Your models learn from it overnight and re-rank tomorrow's queue

Target: 200 activities a daySame reps, about 2.5 times the output

What it does for the business

Each outcome has a measure we'd agree on together before the pilot starts.

Margin back

Rep costs rose about 20% and took margin with them. Tripling what each rep can do is the lever that brings it back, without hiring.

Measure: activities per rep per day, pilot against HubSpot reps

More from Lowe's

Lowe's shares 60,000 records today because HubSpot isn't a certified vendor. On Salesforce, with sharing into Lowe's own org, there's a path past that cap. Proven results there become the case for the next distributors.

Measure: records exposed, verified incremental revenue

One pipe, each brand's flavor

ClickHouse feeds one org through Data 360, so a Toolbelt update reaches reps the same day instead of after 14 days of brand-by-brand work.

Measure: time from data update to rep

Proof for clients
